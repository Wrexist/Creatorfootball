Take over a club in the world's first creator football league — where footballers share a dressing room with the internet's biggest creators.

Creator Football is a premium football management game built for real life: ten-to-fifteen-minute sessions, matches that finish in thirty in-game minutes, and a season you can actually complete. No energy. No timers. No pay-to-win.

YOU TAKE OVER A TWELVE-CLUB LEAGUE
Every club, player, creator, sponsor and broadcaster is original fiction, invented for this universe. You inherit a squad, a stadium and a fanbase with opinions — and twenty-two matches to prove the board right.

RECRUIT FOOTBALLERS AND CREATORS
Sign the clinical finisher your shape needs and the streamer your feed needs. Creators bring reach: bigger crowds, louder sponsors, a social feed that reacts to how you actually play. Footballers bring points. You need both, and you cannot afford both.

MATCHDAY IS THREE DECISIONS
Watch an animated match unfold live. At the moments that decide it, make genuinely difficult calls — press now or protect the lead? Hook your creator for a footballer at 2–1 up? There are no fifty meaningless choices; there are two or three that matter.

RUN THE BUSINESS BETWEEN MATCHES
Transfers with real negotiation. Training that develops players along paths you choose. Facilities, sponsors, fans and media — every pound moving through one auditable ledger, so the economy stays honest and so does yours.

A WORLD THAT KEEPS LIVING
Rivals hire managers with their own philosophies. Veterans retire. Wonderkids arrive. The press writes about what actually happened, not filler. Across seasons you build not just a team but a dynasty — records, legends, and a trophy room worth filling.

TACTICS ARE TRADE-OFFS
Thirty-three tactical settings, each with a stated price. High press wins the ball and burns the legs. A high line suffocates counters and gets punished by pace. There is no dominant stack — only shapes that suit your squad and your nerve.

MADE FOR THE PHONE
iPhone-first design with haptic feedback on every key moment, an interface measured down to the pixel at phone widths, and offline play throughout. Your save lives on your device — no account required.
