Your club. Your story.

Build an original football club in a world of fictional players, creators and rivals. Pick your team, shape its tactics and make the decisions that turn a small club into a football institution.

BUILD YOUR TEAM
Choose your starting lineup and bench, explore formations and tactical instructions, and plan training around development, fitness and availability. Scout players, negotiate transfers and balance ambition against your wage bill.

TAKE CHARGE ON MATCHDAY
Prepare for your opponent, then play or simulate. Follow the score, commentary and match statistics, change tactics and make substitutions as the game unfolds. Review the result and its effects on your squad, supporters and finances.

MAKE YOUR CLUB A HOME
Upgrade facilities and explore an interactive 3D campus that grows with your club. Rotate and zoom the stadium, inspect kits and trophies, and build a history across seasons. An illustration fallback and reduced-effects settings keep the club accessible.

A CREATOR FOOTBALL WORLD
Handle sponsors, fan expectations and press decisions. Follow fictional social posts and creator stories that react to events in your career. All clubs, players, creators and sponsors are original fiction. The social feed is part of the game, with no public chat or user-to-user messaging.

YOUR CAREER STAYS WITH YOU
Play your career offline. No game account or cloud save is required. Export and import career backups through Local saves; keep an exported copy before reinstalling or changing devices.

FREE TO PLAY, OPTIONAL EXTRAS
The complete career is free. Nothing is sold as a random paid reward or loot box, and prices appear in your store's local currency before you confirm.

One-time packs: Club Nights, Heritage and Creator Stories add campus lighting, kit and trophy finishes, and extra storytelling. They are cosmetic and story content with no gameplay stat boosts, and they are yours to keep.

Creator Club: an optional auto-renewing membership with weekly, monthly and yearly plans. Every plan includes the same benefits:
• One welcome signing per career: Kai Arden, a 90-rated striker on a normal in-game contract
• £250,000 in-game club funds to claim each calendar month
• A monthly collaboration with an established fictional creator
• The member library: new commentary and club-journal stories each month
• Exclusive stadium lighting for your campus
Any free trial includes the library and lighting; the signing, club funds and creator collaborations unlock during a paid period. Unclaimed monthly rewards do not carry over. Claimed rewards stay in your career after membership ends. In-game funds have no cash value.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm. Membership renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. Any free trial, and the price after it, is shown before you confirm.

Purchasing and restoring requires an internet connection. Restore packs and membership with the same Apple Account; restoring purchases does not restore career progress.
