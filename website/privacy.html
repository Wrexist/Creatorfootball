Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026 · Applies to the Creator Football iOS app and this website.

Short version: we collect nothing. Your save lives on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs and no third-party trackers.

1. Who we are

Creator Football ("the game") is published by Wrexist ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and this website handle, and it is written to match the App Store privacy label exactly. If the two ever disagree, this policy wins until we fix the label.

2. Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No name, email address or account of any kind — the game has no sign-in.
  • No precise or coarse location.
  • No identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, device fingerprints).
  • No purchase history — there is nothing to buy in Season One.
  • No advertising. No ad SDKs are embedded.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores your save — clubs, squads, fixtures, results, settings — in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share a save file yourself (for example, when emailing support to reproduce a bug). Deleting the app deletes this data.

4. This website

This website is static HTML served by GitHub Pages. We do not set cookies, run analytics or embed third-party scripts on it. GitHub's own infrastructure logging applies when you fetch pages; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

5. Children

The game is rated for ages 4+ and contains no user-generated content sharing, chat or external links to social networks. Because we collect no data at all, no data about children is collected.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies: because we do not collect or process personal data, no legal basis is required. If a future version adds optional telemetry, we will update this policy, seek consent where needed, and update the App Store label before that version ships.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal data. As we hold none, there is nothing to action — but if you believe otherwise, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. Your game save is under your control at all times via deleting the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and, where the change affects the game itself, note it in the App Store "What's New" text for the release that introduces it.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@creatorfootball.app or GitHub Issues.
