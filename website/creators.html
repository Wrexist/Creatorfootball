Get in the game.
Play as yourself.

Creator Football is a league of footballers and creators. If you make things — video, streams, football content, anything with an audience — you can be one of the signable, playable creators managers scout, bid for and build a dynasty around.

Apply to be a creator How it works

What being in the game means

You become a character in the league — not a logo in a credits screen.

🪪

Your own player card

Name, look, position and a set of ratings tuned to how you actually play — plus the reach stat that makes clubs want you.

🤝

Signable by any manager

You appear in the transfer market from Season One. Players scout you, bid for you, and build a shape around you.

🎙️

Moments written for you

Matchday events, press lines and rivalries drawn from your on-camera personality — written with you, not about you.

✍️

You approve everything

Nothing ships without your sign-off. We agree the likeness, the stats and the wording in writing before your card goes live.

Who we're looking for

There's no follower threshold. There is a bar for fit.

You make things regularly

An active channel, feed, stream or podcast — any platform, any size, any language.

Football is somewhere in it

You play, you watch, you talk about it, or your audience does. A cameo works too.

You're up for the bit

Being scouted, benched and sold by strangers is the fun. Bring a sense of humour about it.

Questions creators ask

Does it cost anything?

No. Being in the game is free, and we never ask a creator to pay for placement.

Do I need a big following?

No. The league needs range — breakout names and small, sharp channels both. Fit beats follower count.

Who owns my likeness?

You do. We license the use you agree to, for the game, and you can withdraw it — we'll retire the card in the next update.

What if I'd rather be a club, not a player?

Tell us in the form. Creator-owned clubs and sponsors are both possible in the league fiction.

When would I appear?

Cards agreed before launch appear in Season One. After that, new creators arrive with the seasonal squad updates.
