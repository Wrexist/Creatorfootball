Frequently asked questions

Where is my save stored?

On your device only. Creator Football has no accounts and no servers at launch: your club, squad and season history live in local storage on your phone. Deleting the app deletes the save.

I lost my save. Can you restore it?

If the game detected a damaged save it will have offered to load your most recent backup automatically — look for the "Recovered from a backup" message. Without iCloud or another device backup there is unfortunately nothing to restore from; saves never leave your device.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on-device: matches, transfers, training, the economy and the social feed. No connection required after install.

How long is a season?

Twelve clubs, twenty-two league matches, thirty-minute short-format games. A full season is a few hours of play; individual sessions run ten to fifteen minutes.

Is there pay-to-win?

No. No energy, no timers, no stat boosts for money. What you win, you win on tactics and decisions.

Are the clubs and players real?

No — and that's deliberate. Every club, player, creator, sponsor and competition is original fiction invented for the game. Licensed real-world creators may arrive later as clearly-gated optional packs, never as base content.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later. The game is designed portrait-first on iPhone and supports all orientations on iPad.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@creatorfootball.app with your device model, iOS version and what happened. Because every match in Creator Football is deterministic, a seed from your save lets us replay exactly what you saw.
