## Submitted pitch

Creator Football brings the football-management career into an original world of fictional clubs, players and creator personalities. Build a club from its first season: choose a squad, shape tactics, scout signings, manage finances and make live match decisions. The emotional centre is your club becoming a place: a cinematic manager’s office, a growing interactive 3D stadium campus, and a history shaped by results, supporters and rivalries. Creator stories, press and fan reactions connect the decisions between matches. Designed for portrait play, with local offline careers, exportable backups and no game account or public chat. The complete career is free; optional Creator Club subscriptions and one-time collections are clearly disclosed. Reduced-motion/effects options and an illustration fallback support different devices and preferences. TestFlight 1.0.16 (12) is processed. Public launch timing remains subject to final billing qualification, compliance checks and App Review.
